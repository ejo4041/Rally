# Rally language

**Meeting plan**: The objective, topics, intended outcomes and schedule that Rally uses to guide a meeting. The original plan remains distinct from later revisions.

**Live assessment**: Rally's current estimate of progress and risks while a meeting is happening.

**Intervention**: A brief, eligible instruction or question presented to help the meeting make progress.

**Finalized turn**: A completed transcript segment that can serve as evidence, distinct from a changing partial caption.

**Speaker label**: An anonymous voice label within one transcription session. It is not a verified person's identity.

**Meeting report**: The saved retrospective interpretation of the full meeting, including outcomes, scores and improvement suggestions.

**Due-out**: An action emerging from the discussion, with any stated owner and deadline. Missing details remain unresolved.

**Reported agreement**: Agreement identified in transcript evidence by the model. It is not a separate host confirmation.
