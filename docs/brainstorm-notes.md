# Muse meeting moderator — brainstorming notes

Captured from the user's October 28, 2026 brainstorming session. These are ideas, not committed scope.

## Direction and planning instructions

- A super-competent AI meeting moderator using Meta AI services.
- Product name supplied during the planning interview: **Rally**.
- Explore transcription, Spark, Wonder, Model API, Glimmer, Code, SAM 3.1, and other relevant Meta tooling.
- Prefer broad, useful Meta integration while prioritizing a working, demo-ready product quickly.
- Move involved features later in the roadmap.
- Interview the user before implementation; then create a one-week roadmap and kick off research.
- Save answers and notes to files as the interview progresses; support handoff using Matt Pocock skills.

## Before the meeting

- Paste an agenda or review it verbally before the meeting.
- Set the tone and meeting length.
- Feed in agenda and allotted time.
- Introduce speakers so names are correct.
- Consider whether the meeting could be an email, including during planning.
- Potentially skip unnecessary 1:1 meetings.

## During the meeting

- Kick things off in less than one minute.
- Explain how the moderator will hold the meeting accountable.
- Optionally reserve opening and closing time, e.g. three-minute warm-up and cooldown.
- Transcribe; maintain the correct tone.
- Put “could this meeting be an email?” on the table when appropriate.
- Host override.
- “Good idea fairy” (meaning to clarify).
- Coach on interruptions or someone speaking too quietly.
- Auto-message for hot mic, mute/unmute, etc.

## Wrap-up and follow-through

- Automatically start wrapping up with five minutes remaining.
- Remind people who need to stick around.
- Put together due-outs, follow-up items, and transcript.
- Schedule stick-arounds or automatic breakout rooms at the end.
- Automatically schedule follow-up meetings.
- Give feedback to speakers after the meeting.

## Meeting quality

- Grade the meeting: cost versus value.
- Did participants make decisions? Were there outcomes?
- Track whether meeting quality improves or declines.
- Optional company gamification.

## Delivery and integration ideas

- Completely audio-based assistant invited to calls.
- Mobile phone placed on the table.
- Web app and PWA.
- Assistant joins the meeting as a user.
- Facebook Messenger, WhatsApp, or Teams: choose easiest first integration.
- Calendar, Teams, and Zoom integrations.
