## Available behavior

- Completed Teams transcripts can be saved to meeting history, reloaded, exported and deleted while usage records remain.
- Saved Teams records support host-entered context, retrospective Muse review with explicit permission, evidence-linked proposals, confirmation, dismissal and speaker correction. A speaker correction can dispute affected confirmed outcomes.
- Model selection/pricing is configured by capability through `RALLY_MODEL_CONFIG`. [Interfaces and schema](../model-interfaces.md). Compatible models can reuse an adapter; a new protocol needs an adapter implementation.
- Live Teams audio is transcribed by Muse Voice Transcribe, not Teams' built-in transcription. The server reserves its chosen model and price before the session and preserves that selection through final usage reconciliation.
- Server-approved moderator utterances and a fixed Test voice phrase can use self-hosted Meta MMS through the authenticated speech queue and same-origin browser audio player. Browser/OS voice diagnostics remain off by default; unavailable speech leaves visible text.
- The existing three-minute Teams deadline, consent/admission requirements and guest-browser route remain in place. Speech into the Teams virtual microphone is still pending.

## Services

`rally-mms-runner.service` and `rally-teams-web-runner.service` are manually active. Both remain disabled for automatic startup. The meeting browser is inactive when no job exists. The normal Teams runner was stopped only after verifying no active job, its 30-second lease was allowed to expire, and it was restarted to load the normalized ASR adapter. No stored invite was opened.

The MMS runner verifies/warms the configured checkpoint before polling. It polls idle work once per second and cancellation during generation more frequently. Generated speech has a 60-second access deadline; expired rows are removed on subsequent API/runner requests. Cancellation clears cached audio/text. This is request-driven cleanup, not independent scheduled deletion when every process is stopped.

As `hack`, with `XDG_RUNTIME_DIR=/run/user/1000` if needed:

```bash
systemctl --user status rally-mms-runner.service rally-teams-web-runner.service
systemctl --user start rally-mms-runner.service
```

Check that a service is idle before restarting it. A reboot requires manually starting the selected normal Teams runner and the MMS runner. Credentials remain in the ignored root environment; do not paste their values into commands or documentation.
