## Name

Rally

## Short description

A meeting guide powered by Meta AI that follows the agenda, speaks when useful, and turns discussion into reviewable decisions and next steps.

## Problem and audience

Small software and product groups often finish a meeting with a transcript but still need to establish what was decided, who accepted a task and what remains unresolved. Rally helps the host during that discussion and retains source evidence afterward. It does not infer agreement from a proposed owner or fill in an unspoken deadline.

## Product

Prepare an agenda and a time budget, then invite Rally into Teams or use consented browser audio. Muse Voice Transcribe supplies streaming transcript evidence; Muse Spark 1.3 follows the conversation and agenda, answers direct requests and generates a report from the complete finalized transcript. SeamlessM4T streams approved guidance. The host can pause, dismiss, turn voice off and end the session. Reports retain source references, proposals and unknown details, with named-account read-only sharing and PDF export.

## Meta technology

Muse Voice Transcribe provides ASR; native Teams transcription is not used. Muse Spark 1.3 prepares, continuously evaluates and reviews the meeting. Meta SeamlessM4T-v2-large voices approved interventions through the configured hosted endpoint. Local pinned Meta MMS is a configurable voice and narrates the prepared video. Models remain behind explicit capability interfaces, with provider provenance, cancellation and usage accounting. Living Silk is supplied visual artwork/runtime, not Meta vision inference or participant emotion analysis.

## Technical implementation and current evidence

The application validates evidence revisions and coordinates, keeps live context bounded without truncating stored/report text, separates provisional hypotheses from finalized evidence and rejects stale guidance. A 65-minute actual-provider/local-LiveKit test crossed the normal 55-minute ASR handoff, handled a 25,169-character final turn and delivered all 56,044 canonical characters to the final report. It used authored synthetic speech; it does not establish remote human/Teams acceptance.
