# Rally competitive scan — November 2, 2026

## Scope and conclusion

Quick desk research using vendor product, pricing and support documentation. No accounts created, subscriptions purchased or competitor meetings tested. Features below are vendor-documented, not independently measured. This is a focused competitive scan, not a market-size or willingness-to-pay study.

Transcription, summaries, action extraction and live questions are established features. Live facilitation is also competitive: Microsoft Facilitator covers agendas and time management; Otter documents voice questions; Hedy and Fireflies provide live assistance. Rally should validate a narrower proposition: helping a team reach explicit, evidence-supported decisions and commitments during the meeting, with restrained host-controlled interventions. This is positioning judgment, not proof of uniqueness.

## Read AI

Read supports live transcripts/summaries, engagement/sentiment metrics and in-meeting pace/filler-word feedback. Its private speaker coaching covers clarity, inclusion and impact. These overlap with Rally's live visibility and meeting review, so portraying Read as solely an after-meeting recorder would be inaccurate. [Live tools](https://www.read.ai/meeting-tools), [coaching](https://support.read.ai/hc/en-us/articles/29349563165715-How-does-Read-s-coaching-work).

Ask Read searches connected meeting transcripts, email, chat and other knowledge sources with cited responses. The reviewed documentation describes this as search/knowledge assistance; it does not establish a spoken agenda-moderation participant equivalent to Rally. Absence from these pages is not proof the capability does not exist. [Ask Read FAQ](https://www.read.ai/ask-read/faqs).

Published USD per-user/month pricing: Free5meetings/month; Pro$15annual/$19.75monthly; Enterprise$22.50annual/$29.75monthly; Enterprise+$29.75annual/$39.75monthly. Annual figures mean monthly equivalents under annual billing. Pro includes unlimited transcripts and premium integrations such as Jira/Confluence; playback starts at Enterprise; Enterprise+ adds SAML/SCIM and custom retention. Confirm checkout terms before purchase. [Pricing](https://www.read.ai/plans-pricing).

Read states SOC2TypeII, encryption and opt-in model improvement. Those are vendor claims; this scan does not independently audit them. Do not treat MFA alone as a superior enterprise security posture. Examine actual join/sharing settings and permission boundaries in a trial. [Privacy](https://www.read.ai/privacy), [security overview](https://support.read.ai/hc/en-us/articles/25702259763091-Security-Privacy-Overview).

## Product and Veilant implications — hypotheses

Lead with the meeting outcome, not the transcript: a release condition remains conditional, a proposed owner is not silently converted into an accepted commitment, and unresolved issues remain visible. Rally implements these as behavior requirements and evidence handling, not as a verified competitive accuracy advantage.

A plausible Veilant offer combines facilitated project/change meetings with a reviewed decision/action record that can be handed into Jira/Confluence. Automated system writes and enterprise integrations are future scope, not current shipped capabilities. Sell to team leads/delivery owners facing repeated decision and ownership failures; validate that pain before competing for generic per-seat transcription budgets.

Rally currently provides a guest Teams participant plus browser microphone/tab capture. WhatsApp native guest joining remains experimental. It is not a mature native multi-platform service. Self-hosted application controls are useful, but ASR/reasoning and hosted speech still involve external services; do not claim fully local processing or audited compliance.

## Suggested small comparative trial

Use the same harmless10–15minute release discussion in separate runs with Read, Teams Facilitator when licensed, and Rally. Include a conditional release decision, proposed then accepted ownership, a corrected date, a short tangent, and an addressed question. Measure unsupported commitments, missing owners, correction retention, useful versus unwanted interventions, response delays, and actual recipient report access. These are planned tests, not observed results. Check Read's free meeting/duration allowance and joining/sharing settings before using it. No competitor signup, calendar connection, invitation or paid plan was initiated during this scan.
